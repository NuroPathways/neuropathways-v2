# Replit Prompt: Clean Up NeuroPathways Parent Landing Page

Clean up the NeuroPathways parent-facing landing page so it is simpler, clearer, and focused on parents starting quickly.

The current page should not try to explain every audience or every feature at once. This version should focus on the parent free flow.

Primary goal:

A parent should understand in 5 seconds that NeuroPathways helps them know what to do during stressful behavior moments.

---

## 1. Hero Section

Replace the current hero with this:

Headline:

**What to do in the moment, based on your child’s plan.**

Subheadline:

**Start with one behavior, get simple support steps, and upload your child’s plan later when you’re ready.**

Primary button:

**Start With One Behavior**

Secondary button:

**I Already Have an Account**

Remove extra hero buttons unless needed.

Do not say:

- for clinicians, parents, and clients
- AI document intelligence
- HIPAA conscious
- clinical workflow
- powered by Replit
- crisis app
- therapy replacement

The hero should speak directly to parents only.

---

## 2. How It Works Section

Create a simple three-step section:

### 1. Add your child

Create a quick profile with just a name or nickname.

### 2. Choose one behavior

Start with a common challenge like elopement, meltdowns, aggression, task refusal, or transition difficulty.

### 3. Get support steps

See what to do right now, what to avoid, and what to track.

Keep each step short. No long paragraphs.

---

## 3. Starter Behaviors Section

Add a section titled:

**Start with one common behavior**

Show six clean cards:

1. **Elopement / Running Away**  
   Leaving the area, bolting, or moving toward unsafe places.

2. **Self-Injury**  
   Head hitting, biting, scratching, or other unsafe self-directed behavior.

3. **Aggression**  
   Hitting, kicking, biting, throwing, or unsafe physical behavior.

4. **Meltdowns**  
   Crying, screaming, shutting down, or becoming overwhelmed.

5. **Task Refusal**  
   Refusing homework, chores, hygiene, or other non-preferred tasks.

6. **Transition Difficulty**  
   Struggling to stop one activity and move to the next.

Do not include the full support plan on the landing page. Only show short descriptions.

Add button below:

**Start With One Behavior**

---

## 4. Upload Later Section

Add a section titled:

**Have a plan? Upload it later.**

Text:

**You can start with starter support now. When you’re ready, upload a behavior plan, treatment plan, school support plan, BIP, FBA, or safety plan so NeuroPathways can make the steps more personalized.**

Button:

**Upload a Plan Later**

Do not make upload feel required.

---

## 5. Trust / Privacy Section

Replace vague trust language with:

**Your notes stay inside your private account. Starter support is general guidance until you upload a plan or connect with a clinician.**

Do not claim full HIPAA compliance unless the app is actually operating in a HIPAA-compliant production environment.

Remove:

- HIPAA conscious
- HIPAA ready
- secure medical-grade AI
- powered by Replit
- Replit preview references

Use a custom domain when available.

---

## 6. Visual Cleanup

Make the page feel calm, modern, and parent-friendly.

Use:

- soft blue and green colors
- large rounded cards
- large clear buttons
- more white space
- short sections
- mobile-first layout
- simple icons if available
- no clutter
- no clinical jargon

Reduce text density across the page.

Every section should have one clear purpose.

---

## 7. Parent Account Flow

Make sure the primary CTA goes to parent account creation.

Parent signup should allow:

- parent first name
- email
- password
- confirm password

After signup, route to Parent Quick Start:

1. Add child
2. Choose behavior
3. Choose goal
4. See starter support card

Do not require:

- clinician invite
- clinic code
- treatment plan upload
- diagnosis
- insurance
- long intake form

---

## 8. Final Goal

The cleaned landing page should make one promise:

**NeuroPathways helps parents know what to do in the moment.**

A parent should be able to:

1. understand the product in 5 seconds
2. create an account
3. add a child
4. choose one behavior
5. get a starter support card
6. upload a plan later

Keep the page simple, parent-first, and action-focused.
