# Add-On Prompt: Make Parent-Direct Mode Fully Functional Without a Clinician

Important clarification: this version of NeuroPathways must be able to work without a clinician.

Do not make the parent app dependent on clinician approval, clinician invites, clinician uploads, clinician dashboards, or clinic connection.

The app should support two valid modes:

1. Parent-Direct Mode
2. Clinic-Connected Mode

Both modes must work.

Parent-Direct Mode is not a broken or limited placeholder. It should be a usable version of the app for parents who create their own account, add their own child, use starter support, upload their own documents, and get plan-based support from those uploads.

---

## 1. Parent-Direct Mode Must Work Independently

Parents should be able to use NeuroPathways without a clinician.

Parent should be able to:

- create their own account
- add a child profile
- access starter behavior support
- add supplemental behaviors
- use I Need Help Now
- view Support Now
- use daily activities
- use Prepare flow
- practice skills
- log what happened
- upload their own documents
- scan uploaded documents
- get tasks and activities from uploaded documents
- use AI Chat based on starter support and uploaded documents
- view goals extracted from uploaded documents
- track progress
- delete/request deletion of their data if available

Do not require:

- clinician invite
- clinic code
- clinician approval
- clinician-uploaded plan
- assigned provider
- clinic account
- referral ID

---

## 2. Change the Support Source Hierarchy

Use this hierarchy when deciding what content to show:

1. **Clinician reviewed**
2. **Clinician-uploaded plan**
3. **Parent-uploaded plan**
4. **Starter support**
5. **General safety/emergency guidance**

This means that if no clinician exists, the app still works using:

- starter support
- parent-uploaded documents
- parent-created goals
- parent logs
- parent-selected behaviors

Do not block the parent experience just because clinician approval is missing.

---

## 3. Label Content Correctly Instead of Blocking It

If content is not clinician-reviewed, do not hide it automatically.

Label it clearly.

Use these labels:

- `Starter support`
- `Based on your uploaded plan`
- `Not clinician-reviewed`
- `Clinician reviewed`
- `Published by your care team`

For parent-direct mode, use:

**Based on your uploaded plan · not clinician-reviewed**

or:

**Starter support**

Do not use:

**Clinician reviewed**

unless a clinician actually reviewed it.

---

## 4. Parent-Direct Home Page

In Parent-Direct Mode, the Home page should still show:

1. Support for John
2. I Need Help Now
3. This Week
4. Prepare
5. Today’s Activities
6. Practice This Skill
7. Quick Log
8. Behavior Support Library preview

But the content should come from:

- starter behavior library
- parent-selected behaviors
- parent-uploaded documents
- extracted goals
- recent logs

If there is no uploaded plan yet, show starter support.

If there is an uploaded plan, upgrade the content to:

**Based on your uploaded plan · not clinician-reviewed**

---

## 5. Parent-Direct “This Week”

If there is no clinician handoff, still show a useful This Week section.

Example:

**This week:** Practice one small support step during calm moments.

Source options:

- starter support
- parent-uploaded plan
- parent-selected behavior
- recent logs

Do not require a clinician session summary.

If no plan is uploaded, say:

**This is starter support based on the behavior you selected. Upload a plan later for more personalized support.**

---

## 6. Parent-Direct Prepare Flow

The Prepare flow should work without a clinician.

If no clinician-approved plan exists, use the best available source.

Example:

Source: Starter support

**Prepare for homework**

1. Make the first step small.
2. Offer a simple choice.
3. Plan a short break after effort.

Label:

`Starter support`

If parent uploaded a plan, use that plan.

Label:

`Based on your uploaded plan · not clinician-reviewed`

Do not say:

**Your clinician recommends**

unless the content actually came from a clinician-reviewed source.

Use neutral language:

**Your current support plan says...**

or:

**Your uploaded plan includes...**

or:

**Starter support suggests...**

---

## 7. Parent-Direct Support Now

Support Now must work without a clinician.

If the parent has no uploaded plan, use starter behavior support.

If the parent has uploaded documents, use the uploaded document support.

If the content is safety-critical and no safety plan exists, show:

**No safety plan has been uploaded for this child. If there is immediate danger, call 911 or emergency services.**

Do not invent safety-critical guidance.

But for routine support, the app should still provide starter support.

Example:

**Task Refusal**

Source: `Starter support`

Step 1: Make the task smaller.  
Step 2: Offer two simple choices.  
Step 3: Praise the first attempt.  
Step 4: Log what happened.

---

## 8. Parent-Direct Activities

Activities should work without a clinician.

Activities can be generated from:

- selected behavior
- selected goal
- starter behavior library
- parent-uploaded plan
- recent logs

Examples:

- First-Then Practice
- Calm Space Practice
- Ask for a Break
- Transition Countdown
- Safety Stop Game
- Complete One Small Step

Each activity should show its source:

`Starter support`

or:

`Based on your uploaded plan · not clinician-reviewed`

---

## 9. Parent-Direct AI Chat

AI Chat should work without a clinician.

AI Chat can answer from:

- starter support
- parent-uploaded documents
- behavior library
- goals
- logs

If no clinician-reviewed content exists, it should say:

**This answer is based on starter support or your uploaded information. It has not been reviewed by a clinician.**

If no source exists, say:

**I don’t see plan-based guidance for that yet. I can suggest a general starter activity, or you can upload a plan for more personalized support.**

Do not say:

**Ask your clinician**

as the only answer unless the question involves safety, diagnosis, medication, crisis, or clinical decision-making.

---

## 10. Parent-Uploaded Documents Should Power the App

When a parent uploads a document, the app should scan it and use it to improve the parent experience.

Extract:

- behaviors
- goals
- triggers
- prevention steps
- support strategies
- reinforcement ideas
- safety instructions if present
- activities to practice
- things to log

Then update:

- This Week
- Prepare
- Today’s Activities
- Practice This Skill
- Support Now
- Behavior Support Library
- Goals
- AI Chat

Label all generated content:

**Based on your uploaded plan · not clinician-reviewed**

---

## 11. Clinician Approval Is Optional in Parent-Direct Mode

Clinician approval should improve trust and unlock clinician-reviewed labels, but it should not be required for the app to function.

Do not create logic that prevents parent-direct users from using:

- Support Now
- activities
- tasks
- goals
- AI Chat
- logs
- document upload
- plan extraction

just because there is no clinician.

Instead, use clear labeling and safety limits.

---

## 12. Clinic-Connected Mode Still Exists

Do not remove clinician functionality.

Clinic-connected mode should still support:

- clinician uploads
- clinician review
- approve/publish
- event summaries
- fidelity review
- clinician dashboard
- care team labels

But this should be an additional mode, not a requirement for the parent-direct version.

---

## 13. Adjust Language Based on Mode

If Parent-Direct Mode:

Use:

- your uploaded plan
- starter support
- support plan
- not clinician-reviewed
- upload a plan for more personalized support

Avoid:

- your clinician recommends
- your care team approved
- clinician-reviewed
- published by your provider

unless actually true.

If Clinic-Connected Mode:

Use:

- reviewed by your clinician
- published by your care team
- clinician-approved support
- your care team recommends

only when accurate.

---

## 14. Safety Rules Still Apply

Even in Parent-Direct Mode:

- do not invent crisis instructions
- do not invent medication guidance
- do not diagnose
- do not create a new treatment plan
- do not claim clinician review
- do not give unsafe instructions

For immediate danger:

**If there is immediate danger, call 911 or emergency services.**

For missing safety plan:

**No safety plan has been uploaded for this child. Upload a safety plan or contact a qualified professional for individualized guidance.**

---

## 15. Final Requirement

Do not make NeuroPathways dependent on clinicians for this version.

This app must be useful for parents on its own.

The parent-direct version should let a parent:

1. create an account
2. add a child
3. select behaviors
4. access starter support
5. use I Need Help Now
6. use tasks and activities
7. upload documents
8. generate support from uploaded documents
9. log what happened
10. use AI Chat with clear source labels

The clinician-connected version should add review, approval, publishing, fidelity monitoring, and care-team feedback.

But the parent-direct version must be fully usable without a clinician.
