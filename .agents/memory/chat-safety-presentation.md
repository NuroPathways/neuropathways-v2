name: Chat safety presentation
description: Safe boundaries for progressive disclosure in parent AI Chat.
---

Keep immediate-danger guidance and the short medical-advice notice visible. Without trusted structured safety classification, keep complete model-returned answers and disclaimers outside collapsed sections.

**Why:** The user permits compact nonurgent explanations but explicitly forbids hiding emergency warnings or rewriting clinical advice. Arbitrary returned text may contain urgent information.

**How to apply:** Collapse only known static nonurgent introduction text. Sources may use optional disclosure while retaining all supplied titles, excerpts and identifiers. Preserve existing provenance/review criteria and backend safety behavior; do not infer urgency or clinician approval from a presentation heuristic.
