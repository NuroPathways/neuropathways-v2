---
name: Legal-notice provenance
description: Preserve the meaning of recorded policy acceptances when updating legal explanations.
---

Do not silently rewrite a versioned policy that existing consent records identify. Supplemental implementation explanations should be clearly separate from the accepted policy unless a coordinated policy-version change is approved.

**Why:** Legal-copy work must not retroactively make an account's historical acceptance refer to text the user never saw. The operator also restricted this review to informational changes, not new consent behavior.

**How to apply:** Check the stored consent/version contract before modifying policy text. Use a separately labeled feature notice for implementation disclosures. Substantive policy adoption, version changes, renewed consent, and operational rights-request processes require explicit scope and operator/legal review.
