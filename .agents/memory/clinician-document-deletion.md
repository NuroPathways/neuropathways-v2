---
name: Clinician deletion of family-uploaded documents
description: User-approved permission and scope for clinician deletion of a client's family-uploaded document.
---

An assigned clinician may delete a family-uploaded document attached to a client in their own caseload. Deletion removes the source document from both clinician and family document centers; separately created goals, notes, and support information remain.

**Why:** The user approved clinician deletion after confirming its effect on the family account.

**How to apply:** Confirm before deletion, authorize only the clinician assigned to that child (and only documents explicitly shared with them), and preserve cross-clinician and unshared-document restrictions.
